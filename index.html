Every HVAC business publicly listed for sale is evaluated and filtered to the SBA sweet spot — $500K–$3M asking price, field-service businesses only.

Each deal runs through the same SBA 7(a) math a lender will — 12.5% down, current market rate, 10-year term, and a 20% stressed SDE haircut before DSCR is recalculated.
